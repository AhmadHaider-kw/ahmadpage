MY PROCESS

Idea to cast

ONE

Idea

The subject, the composition, the scale — drawn and agreed before any clay is touched.

TWO

Clay

Modelled by hand over an armature. Still soft — the last point of free revision.

THREE

Mould

Silicone and plaster. The clay is lost; the form becomes permanent.

FOUR

Cast

Cast in bronze, chased and finished. Patina, mounting, delivery.
