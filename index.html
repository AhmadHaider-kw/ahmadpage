I'm a sculptor based in Kuwait. I make portraits, relief, and figures — modelled by hand in the traditional sequence, and cast in bronze, ceramic, and plaster. Trained intensively at the Florence Academy of ArtItaly and the Dutch Academy of Figurative SculptureAmsterdam, under master sculptors.

PROCESS

Image to cast

ONE

Image

Sittings, photographs, measurements. Scale and material fixed.

TWO

Clay

Built over an armature. Still soft — your last point of free revision.

THREE

Mould

Silicone and plaster. The clay is lost; the form becomes permanent.

FOUR

Cast

Lost wax bronze, chased and finished. Patina, mounting, delivery.
